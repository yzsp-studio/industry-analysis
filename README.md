面向行业中长期基本面与产业研究的深度技能，覆盖行业定义与规模、产业链与竞争格局、政策与驱动力、景气周期、趋势研判与三情景、盈利质量与落地建议。它要求先写出一段几百字的「判断主线」底稿，明确这份报告要证明、且可被推翻的核心判断，再用三级数据分级取证，按固定五大板块写透。进入正文或表格的每个数字都要可溯源，区分一手披露、具名机构、行业数据与自身测算，绝不靠印象或模糊出处补数字；任何让报告变浅、变得不可溯源或更像填空模板的排版规则，都要给质量标准让路。最终交付一份可直接用于战略规划、投资决策与商业化落地的飞书深度报告。

## 🇬🇧 English

A deep-research skill for a sector's mid- to long-term fundamentals and industrial landscape: market definition and size, value chain and competitive dynamics, policy and drivers, cycle position, trend judgement with three scenarios, earnings quality and actionable recommendations. It forces a few-hundred-word 'thesis mainline' first — the falsifiable judgement the report has to prove — then gathers evidence under a three-tier data grading scheme and writes a fixed five-section report. Every number that reaches the body or a table must be traceable, with first-hand disclosure, named institutions, industry data and own estimates kept clearly apart; nothing is filled in from impressions or vague sourcing. Any formatting rule that makes the report shallower or less traceable yields to the quality bar. The output is a Feishu deep-dive report usable for strategy, investment and commercialisation decisions.

### ✨ What it does

- Produces a few-hundred-word thesis mainline before writing the five fixed sections
- Covers market definition and size, value chain, competitive landscape and policy drivers
- Three-tier data grading: every number carries a source and a confidence level
- Cycle positioning, trend judgement and three-scenario projection
- Earnings quality, cash flow and actionable recommendations tied to decision impact
- Rendered as a deliverable Feishu report, with formatting always serving analytical depth
